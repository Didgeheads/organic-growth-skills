# GEO content recce (Botworthy Score)

The current version of this skill is free by email from https://botworthyindex.com. It is no longer kept in this repository, so that everyone runs the same, current version of the score.

Created by Jessica Redman (jmredman.com), creator of the Botworthy Score. The Botworthy Score is a 0 to 100 score for how likely AI models are to quote a page, across 10 criteria weighted by published research into what AI models actually cite.

## What the skill does
- Scores 25 to 40 articles from one section of a site against the 10-criterion Botworthy Score.
- Codes every section opening twice (answer-first, deferral or lead-in; self-contained or not) and measures agreement between coders.
- Runs significance and robustness tests, classifies the writing habits behind weak sections, and writes a client-facing report with charts and before/after rewrites.

## Where to get it
- The skill, free by email: https://botworthyindex.com
- Every weight and the research behind it: https://botworthyindex.com/scoreboard
- What changed in each release: https://botworthyindex.com/updates
